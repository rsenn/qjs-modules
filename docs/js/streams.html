#streams

Source: lib/streams.js (pure JS)

Higher-level helpers built on top of the WHATWG stream implementation — file-backed streams, line iteration, and text transform streams. Re-exports everything from lib/stream.js (the full WHATWG streams, with pipeTo()/pipeThrough()).
